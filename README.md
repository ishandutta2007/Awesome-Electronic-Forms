# Top Electronic Forms Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS/Commercial Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Online Form Builders, Surveys, Conversational Forms, Data Collection & No-Code Form Creation*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Electronic Forms**. These tools let you create, share, and analyze online forms and surveys — from simple contact forms to complex multi-step, logic-driven, and conversational experiences.

**Examples** include Jotform, Typeform, Formstack, Cognito Forms, Paperform, Fillout, Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Zoho Forms, and Formsite (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted form and survey builders — ideal for teams that want unlimited responses, full data ownership, and freedom from per-submission or per-form pricing.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/)**  
  Feature-rich online form builder with extensive templates, payments, integrations, approval workflows, and a large library of widgets.

- **[Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/)**  
  Leading conversational form platform known for one-question-at-a-time experiences, high completion rates, and polished design.

- **[Formstack](https://www.formstack.com/)**  
  Enterprise-focused form and document automation platform with advanced logic, workflows, and compliance features.

- **[Cognito Forms](https://www.cognitoforms.com/)**, **[Paperform](https://paperform.co/)**, **[Fillout](https://www.fillout.com/)**  
  Modern no-code form builders offering calculations, payments, integrations, and flexible design options.

- **[Google Forms](https://forms.google.com/)**, **[Microsoft Forms](https://forms.microsoft.com/)**, **[Zoho Forms](https://www.zoho.com/forms/)**, **[Formsite](https://www.formsite.com/)**  
  Widely used free or low-cost form tools tightly integrated with their respective productivity ecosystems (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho, etc.).

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Formbricks](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks)**  
  Leading open-source experience management and form/survey platform. Supports link surveys, in-app surveys, website targeting, conditional logic, and easy self-hosting via Docker. Strong privacy and data-ownership focus.

- **[OpnForm](https://github.com/OpnForm/OpnForm)**  
  Beautiful open-source no-code form builder (AGPL) with a polished interface, AI form generation, file uploads, integrations, and unlimited forms/submissions when self-hosted.

- **[HeyForm](https://github.com/heyform/heyform)**  
  Open-source conversational form builder in the Typeform style — one question at a time, clean UX, and fully self-hostable.

- **[Typebot](https://github.com/baptisteArno/typebot.io)**  
  Open-source chatbot-style / conversational form platform with visual flow builder, advanced logic, and strong integration options.

- **[LimeSurvey](https://github.com/LimeSurvey/LimeSurvey)**  
  Mature, battle-tested open-source survey platform (GPL) widely used in academia and research, with deep question-type support and extensive features.

- **[Form.io](https://github.com/formio/formio)**  
  Developer-focused, JSON-schema-driven open-source form and data management platform, ideal for embedding forms into applications (enterprise edition available).

- **[OhMyForm](https://github.com/ohmyform/ohmyform)**  
  Open-source alternative to Typeform and Google Forms for creating, administering, and analyzing forms with self-hosting support.

- **[Additional form & survey projects](https://github.com/)**  
  SurveyJS, Tripetto (SDK), Budibase form capabilities, and other community form builders and libraries.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- WordPress form plugins (Gravity Forms alternatives that are open-source, or free plugins).
- Static-site friendly form backends (Formspree-style open alternatives, Formscale, etc.).
- PDF form filling and e-signature tools (e.g., DocuSeal) that complement electronic forms.
- Many headless form APIs and React/Vue form libraries for custom implementations.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: For modern surveys and experience management start with **Formbricks**. Choose **OpnForm** or **HeyForm** for polished no-code or conversational forms. Use **Typebot** when you need chatbot-style flows. Deploy **LimeSurvey** for research-grade surveys. Embed **Form.io** when forms are part of a larger application. All of the above can be self-hosted with Docker and paired with your own database and email infrastructure.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Form tools often collect personal data. Self-hosted open-source solutions give full control and unlimited responses but require you to handle security, spam protection, backups, email deliverability, and compliance (GDPR, etc.). Commercial platforms typically offer more templates, payment integrations, and managed infrastructure out of the box.
- Always review data retention, privacy policies, and accessibility when deploying forms that collect user information.
